A head that flows into the grip

The blade-drawer concept with its head reshaped. The flat-topped block is gone: above the cap, the head is now one smooth surface that sweeps from the full blade width into the waist of the handle. Everything the blade, cap and guard touch is exactly as in v4.

changed: head above the cap unchanged: sole, dovetails, pegs, snap unchanged: guard fit unchanged: blade drawer

Before and after

Before: a 53 mm wide box with a square back, with the grip attached behind it. After: the box's top corners are rounded off, its back sweeps into the waist in one curve, and the top rises 1.5 mm into the neck instead of stepping. The thumb dish moved 4 mm back so it sits on the new neck.

How it keeps working

The head is split 3.6 mm above the sole, which is 0.4 mm above the top of the cap's lips. Below that line nothing changed. Above it the shape is free, with one exception: under the guard (the front 18 mm), the new top may not rise above v4's nose slope.

The cap and the guard

With the head smoothed, the orange cap's square back corners stand out more. I left the cap alone on purpose: it clamps in its last 2 mm of travel, right at those corners, so rounding them would weaken the clamp you're about to test. Once the v4 test-fit print confirms the clamp, a 1 mm chamfer on the cap's outer top edges is a safe cosmetic change. The guard could get the same treatment.
