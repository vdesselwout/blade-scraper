Your question

Storing spare blades in the handle

Yes. The blades lie flat: 61 mm along the handle and 19 mm across it, which fits inside a 31 mm wide grip with 2 mm walls each side. Stood on edge they would need a 23 mm tall handle, which is taller than any of the new concepts.

How the drawer works

  1. A tray slides into a 23 × 4.8 mm channel that runs 66 mm into the handle from the butt. The drawer's end piece is the rounded tail of the handle.
  2. The tray pocket is blade-shaped, so the blades can't shift. Their cutting edges sit against the tray's solid side wall, never at the open side.
  3. 5 blades stack 3 mm high in a 3.4 mm pocket. A 10 mm hole in the floor lets you push the stack up to pick off the top blade.
  4. A small detent will hold the drawer shut (designed in v5, worth a quick test print). With the drawer in, the blades are fully enclosed.

Printing: the channel roof is a 23 mm bridge, which PLA handles without supports. The drawer prints flat on its floor.

Other ways I looked at

OptionWhy not
Hatch on the undersideThe underside is where your fingers wrap. A seam or latch there is felt every stroke, and the lid can be knocked open.
Blades on edge in a slotNeeds a handle at least 23 mm tall along the whole slot, which works against the slimmer shapes.
Magnet in the handleNeeds hardware, which the brief rules out, and leaves the edges exposed.
Clip on the guardPossible, but then the spares are only with you when the guard is on. Easy to add later on top of any concept.

The drawer can go into concept 1 or 4 too. It doesn't fit with concept 3, because the window takes the space the channel needs.

Recommendation

Concept 2, the blade drawer

It keeps the v4 way of holding the tool, at 21 mm shorter, with a lower and smoother profile, and it adds safe blade storage while still using about 2 g less filament than v4. If storage turns out not to matter, concept 1 is the same shape without the drawer and saves the most for the least risk (about 9 g).

Once you pick one I'll build it as v5 with the drawing sheet, renders, review row and report, plus a small test-fit piece for the drawer detent.
