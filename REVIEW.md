# Blade scraper — review log

Approved version: **v1** (2026-10-03); v3 guard (fits the printed v1) and v4 tapered dovetails pending test prints; v5 (new handle, blade drawer) and v6 (open-top guard) pending review; v7 (guard grips the head) pending a guard test print. Print files are in prints/vN/: v1 body, cap, test-fit piece and the sliced gcode; v3 guard (plus all three parts with the v3 guard); v4 all parts and test-fit project; v5 all four parts and the drawer test-fit project; v6 all four parts, the guard on its own and the guard test-fit project; v7 the same (only the guard changed). Design concepts are in concepts/.

| v3 | Guard: walls relieved to the blade slot's width below the slot; 0.8 mm lead-in chamfer at the mouth | The cap's flared first layers (elephant's foot) caught on one side, and the mouth's own first layers narrow the opening | pending: test print |

Drawer test-fit piece | User wanted it sleeker, shorter and lighter on filament, safe storage for spare blades, and a head that flows into the handle. Chosen from the v5 concept study (concept 2 with the flowing head) | pending: review, then drawer test-fit print |

| v7 | Guard only: the two friction bumps on the cap walls are gone; instead a 45°-ended pad on each rail presses 0.35 mm into the head's flank above the cap (x 12.5–18, z 4.0–4.7, clear of the dovetail band).
